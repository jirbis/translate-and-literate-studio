# PITCH

## translate-and-literate  
### Human-in-the-loop editorial AI by Jirbis GmbH

---

## The problem

High-quality translation is still expensive, slow, and inconsistent.

- Generic AI translators are fluent but generic  
- Human translators preserve meaning but struggle with scale  
- Editorial voice is lost across documents and teams  
- Human corrections are wasted instead of reused  

Most tools optimize for **speed**.  
Professional content requires **authority, consistency, and voice**.

---

## The insight

Translation is not a single action.  
It is an **editorial process**.

The real value is not in the first draft, but in:
- how humans correct it
- how style decisions repeat
- how voice stabilizes over time

Those decisions are usually lost.

---

## The solution

**translate-and-literate** is a human-in-the-loop, agent-orchestrated editorial pipeline that separates:

1. **Meaning-accurate translation**
2. **Literary editing and voice shaping**

Human edits are captured as structured knowledge:
- style rules
- terminology decisions
- editorial examples

The system learns from real editorial work — not from abstract preferences.

---

## Why this matters

- Editorial voice becomes reproducible
- Quality improves with use
- Human effort compounds instead of resetting
- AI accelerates work without replacing judgment

> AI does the work.  
> Humans keep authority.  
> Systems learn from decisions.

# PROMPT: TRANSLATOR

Goal: translate from English to Russian while preserving meaning, structure, and facts.

Instructions:
- Do not polish style—prioritize accurate meaning.
- Use the glossary and the Do Not Translate list.
- Small refactors for readability are allowed, but no literary embellishments.
- Preserve formatting and lists.

Poetry-specific scope:
- If the source is poetry, lyrics or rap, follow `docs/poetry_translation_workflow.md` for a meaning-first draft and optional requested formal variants.
- Do not force rhyme or regular meter by distorting meaning, natural Russian, or the poet's intended irregularity.
- RPST is a Russian-language diagnostic tool; do not claim it has scanned the English original.
